LCD (GC9A01 SPI Display)

LCD Pin	    ESP32 Pin	Notes
    VCC	    3.3V	    Power
    GND	    GND	        Ground
    SCL	    GPIO 18	    SPI Clock (SCK)
    SDA	    GPIO 23	    SPI MOSI (Data)
    CS	    GPIO 5	    Chip Select
    DC	    GPIO 27	    Data/Command
    RST	    GPIO 33	    Reset

Rotary Encoder
(CLK, DT, SW, +, GND):

Encoder Pin	    ESP32 Pin	Notes
        CLK	    GPIO 25	    Rotation signal A
        DT	    GPIO 26	    Rotation signal B
        SW	    GPIO 32	    Push button
        +	    3.3V	    Power
        GND	    GND	        Ground
